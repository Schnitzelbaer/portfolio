## Starting point

Kindred's coordinators plan visits for 140 carers across 600 clients. They worked with spreadsheets and a whiteboard, and every sick call meant rebuilding half a week by hand.

![Weekly roster with automatic gap detection](./images/roster.jpg "Weekly roster. Gaps and conflicts are flagged before the week starts.")

<Decision title="Suggest, never auto-assign">
  Coordinators know their carers and clients personally. The tool proposes replacements ranked by travel time and continuity of care, but a person always confirms.
</Decision>

<Showcase id="care-roster" caption="Covering a sick call in the new roster." />

![Shift handover on the carer's phone](./images/handover.jpg "Handover notes travel with the visit, not with the carer.")

## Outcome

<Outcome type="expected">
  - Planning time down to two hours per week across all pilot teams.
  - Fewer last-minute changes for clients with dementia, where continuity matters most.
</Outcome>
