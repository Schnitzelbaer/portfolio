## Context

Facility managers at Voltaro's customers oversee dozens of buildings each. The old portal showed every meter as a chart, which meant nobody knew where to start.

![Portfolio overview ranking buildings by savings potential](./images/overview.jpg "Portfolio overview, ranked by savings potential instead of consumption.")

<Decision title="Rank by action, not by consumption">
  The biggest building always used the most energy, which taught nobody anything. We ranked buildings by deviation from their own baseline, so the list showed where to act this week.
</Decision>

## Outcome

<Outcome type="achieved">
  - Customers identified 12% savings in the first heating season.
  - Weekly active use of the portal tripled.
</Outcome>
